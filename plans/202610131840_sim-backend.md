# The sim backend

**Status:** In progress

Implements [sim.md](../specs/sim.md) and the parts of [config.md](../specs/config.md), [robot.md](../specs/robot.md), [bridge.md](../specs/bridge.md), [project.md](../specs/project.md), [testing.md](../specs/testing.md) and [ci.md](../specs/ci.md) it changed. It delivers three things: `backend: "sim"`, which starts and owns a naoqi-sim or borrows the one already running (`sim.spawn`); the live tier on a sim by default, with one sim per run; and the `e2e-sim` CI job on NAOqi 2.1 and 2.8. It leaves out touch on a sim and packages installed into a sim (sim.md, open questions).

Prerequisite: [sim.md](../specs/sim.md) promoted from `Draft` to `Stable` after review.

## Scope

- `pyproject.toml`: the `sim` extra (`naoqi-sim[viewer]` with `qi`'s platform marker), `naoqi-sim = { git = "https://github.com/funwithagents/naoqi-sim", rev = "<commit>" }` in `[tool.uv.sources]`, `naoqi-bridge[sim]` in the `dev` group; `uv.lock` regenerated
- `src/naoqi_bridge/config.py`: `Backend` gains `"sim"`; `SimSpawn`, `SimSettings(spawn, config)`; the `sim` loader (`spawn` checked here, `config` through `naoqi_sim.config.NaoqiSimConfig.from_dict` with its key prefixed `sim.config.`; without the extra, a `ConfigError` on `backend: "sim"` only)
- `src/naoqi_bridge/sim.py` (new): `SimLease`, `acquire_sim(settings)` (the own-or-borrow table, version check, boot wait, cancel-safe), `release()`; naoqi-sim errors re-raised as `RobotConnectionError`, chained
- `src/naoqi_bridge/robot.py`: `build_robot` on `sim` returns `NaoqiRobot("127.0.0.1", 9559, …)`
- `src/naoqi_bridge/bridge.py`: `start()` acquires the lease before the robot and releases it on failure or cancel; `stop()` releases it last
- `src/naoqi_bridge/__init__.py`: export `SimSettings`, `SimSpawn`
- `src/naoqi_bridge/naoqi_mcp_server.py`, `naoqi_websocket_server.py`: check that a `RobotConnectionError` from a sim exits 1 like a robot's (no change expected)
- `examples/configs/mcp-sim.json`, `websocket-sim.json`, `README.md` (the folder's)
- `tests/test_config.py`, `tests/test_sim.py` (new), `tests/test_robot.py`, `tests/test_bridge.py`: the fast tier, against naoqi-sim's real config class and a stubbed `read_status` / `NaoqiSim`
- `tests-e2e/support.py`, `tests-e2e/conftest.py`: the target variables, the session sim fixture on its own loop thread, `live_config` per target, motion always on and interactive skipped on a sim
- `tests-e2e/test_live_sim.py` (new): borrow and leave running, `always` refused, the other version refused
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the `e2e-sim` job
- `AGENTS.md`: the project map row for `sim.py`, the testing paragraph (targets)
- `README.md`: the sim backend (install the extra, `fetch-and-build-images`, a sim config, running the live tier on a sim)
- `specs/sim.md` frontmatter: `code:` gains `src/naoqi_bridge/sim.py`; `tests:` gains `tests/test_sim.py`, `tests-e2e/test_live_sim.py`; `specs/testing.md` and `specs/ci.md` frontmatter as files appear

## Steps

1. **Dependency.** Pin naoqi-sim's `main` at implementation time (today `c1f4e02`, Windows live fixes included). `uv lock`, `uv sync`. Check that `uv sync` still works on the platforms without `qi` (the marker leaves naoqi-sim out) and that pyright sees `naoqi_sim`.
2. **Config.** `SimSettings` and its loader, as config.md "The `sim` block". Tests: a naoqi-sim example file pasted as `sim.config` loads; `spawn` defaults to `auto` and rejects other words; a naoqi-sim error comes back with its key under `sim.config.`; `NaoqiBridge("sim")` loads with naoqi-sim's defaults; with `naoqi_sim` made unimportable (monkeypatched import), `backend: "sim"` is a `ConfigError` naming the extra while `backend: "fake"` with a `sim` block loads.
3. **`sim.py`.** `acquire_sim` over `naoqi_sim.read_status()` and `naoqi_sim.NaoqiSim`, imported in the function. Booting means `containers` listed without a ready `naoqi`, polled every 1 s up to `ready_timeout_s`; the version compares `NaoqiSimStatus.naoqi_version` with the config's `2.1` / `2.8` prefix. Fast tests stub both (monkeypatched on the `naoqi_sim` module) and cover every cell of sim.md's table, the boot wait and its timeout, the version mismatch, a `NaoqiSimError` re-raised as `RobotConnectionError` with its message, a cancelled acquire that stops what it started, and `release()` stopping an owned sim only.
4. **Robot and bridge.** `build_robot` on `sim`; the lease in `start()` / `stop()`. Fast tests with the stubbed sim and a stub `qi` (as `test_naoqi_robot.py` does): `start()` starts an owned sim then connects to `127.0.0.1:9559`, `stop()` stops it after closing the robot; a failed connect releases the lease; a borrowed sim survives `stop()`.
5. **Examples and README.** `mcp-sim.json` and `websocket-sim.json`, which `tests/test_config.py`'s example-file test loads like the others.
6. **Live tier.** `support.py`: `TARGET`, `SIM_VERSION`, `SIM_VIEWER` read once, with unknown values raised as `pytest.UsageError`. `live_config()` per target. `require_flag("NAOQI_E2E_ALLOW_MOTION")` applies on `real` only, and the touch test skips on `sim`. `conftest.py`: a session-scoped autouse fixture that, on `sim`, starts a bridge (`spawn: "auto"`, the ci-like or the window settings) on a loop in its own thread, skips the run with naoqi-sim's message when the target is implicit, and fails it when `NAOQI_E2E_TARGET=sim`. `test_live_sim.py` as testing.md describes. The MCP and WebSocket server tests write the target's config for their subprocess.
7. **Local runs.** `uv run naoqi-sim fetch-and-build-images` (2.1 and 2.8), then `uv run pytest tests-e2e -rs` on 2.1, on 2.8, and on 2.1 with `NAOQI_E2E_SIM_VIEWER=1`. Fix what fails in the bridge. A 2.8 gap in `NaoqiRobot` is fixed if it is small; otherwise it is recorded in robot.md's open question and the test is marked as an expected skip on 2.8 in ci.md, with the reason. Record the measured skips in ci.md's "Expected skips" and the timings below.
8. **CI.** The `e2e-sim` job as ci.md describes. Push, check both entries cold and then from the cache, and record their timings in ci.md.
9. **Statuses.** Mark sim.md and the `Updated` specs `Implemented`, update `_overview.md`'s status table and this plan to `Done`, in the files and the indexes.
